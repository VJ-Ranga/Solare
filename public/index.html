Specified Skilled Worker (SSW)

Japan's main route for skilled workers in 16 industries. Pass a skills test and a Japanese test, and work up to five years — longer with SSW (ii).

  • Skills test + JLPT N4 / JFT-Basic
  • Same pay as Japanese staff
  • Change employer within your field

How it works 流れ

Five clear steps from Sri Lanka to Japan

We explain every document and every cost before you decide. No surprises.

  1. Free consultation

    Visit us or message on WhatsApp. We check your skills, age and goals and suggest the right programme.

  2. Japanese training

    Classes for JLPT N5–N4 / JFT-Basic, plus workplace manners and daily-life Japanese.

  3. Skills test & interview

    We prepare you for the skills test and online interviews with Japanese employers.

  4. Visa & documents

    Certificate of Eligibility, visa, SLBFE registration and medicals — handled step by step.

  5. Fly & settle in

    Pre-departure briefing, airport pick-up and a contact person in Japan after you arrive.
